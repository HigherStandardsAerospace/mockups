OUR STORY

Founder-owned since 2017

Higher Standards Aerospace is an FAA Part 145 repair station in Doral, Florida, specializing in the test, repair and overhaul of aircraft fuel, engine, APU and airframe components. Frank Villasante founded HSA in 2017. He acquired the business, relocated it, and expanded it into the 25,000 square foot facility we operate today. Before HSA he owned High Standard Aviation, and he brought the same standard with him.

What family owned actually means here

A lot of shops in this industry sit inside private equity portfolios or run as one division of a bigger group. We don’t. HSA is family owned and family operated, so the people deciding how your unit gets handled are the people whose name is on the building. That shows up in ordinary ways. Decisions don’t travel through three approval layers, and when something goes wrong you’re talking to someone who can fix it the day you call.

A specialist shop by choice

We could have added component families over the years. We went deeper instead. Fuel, engine, APU and airframe accessories are the whole business, and staying in that lane is what lets us keep test capability in house and track how our own work holds up after it ships.

The people doing the work

Our technicians are highly specialized and work under continuous education and training programs. Specialized isn’t a courtesy word here. Our technicians work the same component families year after year instead of moving between unrelated disciplines, and that familiarity is most of what a customer is paying for. It’s also what makes the reliability tracking worth anything. Consistent hands on consistent components is what lets a pattern in the data mean something.

Where we work

Our customers are in the United States, Europe, the United Kingdom, Latin America and Asia, supported from a single facility in Doral, Florida.
