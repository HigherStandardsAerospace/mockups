FAA, EASA, UK CAA and CAAC approved

Higher Standards Aerospace is an FAA Part 145 repair station. We also hold EASA, UK CAA and CAAC approvals, which means components released by HSA carry documentation accepted by operators in the United States, Europe, the United Kingdom and China.

What four approvals mean in practice

FAA Part 145 is the baseline for any repair station releasing components in the United States. The other three widen where a unit from HSA can go. EASA and UK CAA approvals mean a component can be accepted by operators across Europe and the United Kingdom without a further release. CAAC covers China. For an airline or a tier 1 MRO working across regions, that removes a question about where the unit can ultimately be installed. For a trader it widens the pool of buyers who can take the asset, which is the difference between inventory that moves and inventory that sits.

How we work to them

All work is performed strictly in accordance with the manufacturer’s manuals and specifications and FAA approved data. Our technicians are highly specialized and work under continuous education and training programs, and our quality department maintains reliability tracking on every part number we support.

Test stands
