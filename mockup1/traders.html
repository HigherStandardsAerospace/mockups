Four approvals travel with the asset

HSA holds FAA Part 145, EASA, UK CAA and CAAC approvals. A component released by us carries documentation accepted by operators in the United States, Europe, the United Kingdom and China, which widens the pool of buyers who can take it.

Reliability history you can pass on

Because we track performance by part number, an overhauled unit from HSA comes with more than a release. It comes with a shop that can speak to how that part number performs in service, which is something you can put in front of your own buyer.

Known for one thing

Buyers weigh risk. A unit released by a shop known for fuel, engine and APU accessories carries less of it than the same unit from a general repair station, and that shows up in how quickly it moves.
