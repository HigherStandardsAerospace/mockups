Engine fuel control repair and overhaul

Higher Standards Aerospace repairs and overhauls engine fuel control units for the CFM56 and V2500 platforms. Every unit is disassembled, cleaned, inspected, assembled and tested on our fuel control test stands in Doral, Florida.

What this component does

The engine fuel control unit meters fuel flow to the combustor in response to power lever position and to the operating conditions the engine is seeing at the time. It is a hydromechanical device working to tight internal tolerances, which is why wear that would be unremarkable in a larger assembly shows up here first as a scheduling problem and eventually as a removal. Because the unit governs fuel scheduling directly, a discrepancy rarely stays contained. What gets reported as a fuel control issue often arrives with symptoms elsewhere, so part of what we do on receipt is establish what the unit itself is responsible for before a workscope is agreed.

How we process it

Every unit is disassembled, cleaned, inspected, assembled and tested on our test stands H.S.A Doral. All work performed strictly in accordance with the manufacturer’s manuals and specifications and FAA approved data. Testing happens in our own building. The unit does not go out to a subcontractor and does not wait in another shop’s queue.

Reliability on this part number

We track in-service performance on every part number we support, including this one. When you send us this unit we can tell you how the part number has behaved after previous releases from our shop, not only that it passed acceptance test on the day it shipped.
