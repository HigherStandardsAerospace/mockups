Our story

Built around
specialist work.

Founded in 2017 by Frank Villasante, Higher Standards Aerospace is a family-owned and operated business focused on aircraft component repair and overhaul.

HSA brings fuel, HMU, hydraulic, APU and airframe component capability together in Doral. Our facility supports disassembly, cleaning, inspection, assembly and testing.
